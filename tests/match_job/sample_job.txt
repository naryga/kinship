Senior Data Engineer
Tessellate Health - Faketown, Example State (hybrid)

About the role
Tessellate Health processes pharmacy and medical claims for regional health plans. We are
looking for a Senior Data Engineer to own the data pipelines that move claims from payers
into our reporting warehouse, and to make healthcare billing data something the rest of
the company can trust.

What you will do
- Design, build and operate batch data pipelines in Apache Airflow, with alerting and a
  runbook for every job
- Model claims and billing data in dbt and keep the Snowflake warehouse fast and well tested
- Add data quality checks that catch schema drift and rejected claims before they reach
  finance dashboards
- Work with our compliance team on HIPAA audit trails and access reviews
- Mentor other engineers and take part in the incident review rota

What we are looking for
- 5+ years of data engineering experience with Python and SQL
- Production experience with Airflow or a similar orchestrator, and with dbt
- Experience with streaming systems such as Kafka or Spark is a plus
- Background in healthcare billing, pharmacy claims or insurance payers
- Clear written communication with stakeholders outside engineering

Benefits
Competitive salary and equity, health insurance, a remote-friendly team and a generous
holiday allowance. Tessellate Health is an equal opportunity employer.
